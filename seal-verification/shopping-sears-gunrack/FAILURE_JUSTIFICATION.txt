The model gives the wrong cash price for the Western-style Gun Rack on printed page 416 of the 1959 Sears Christmas Book. That listing (teaches neatness; stained pine with burned-in western design; eight hooks for four rifles; three pegs for hats or pistols; about 20 inches wide and 20 1/2 inches high; partly assembled; catalog 79 N 02683) is priced at 3.39 dollars. Reporting 1.98 dollars, 2.98 dollars, 3.19 dollars, or any nearby toy-gun price from the same page is incorrect. WishbookWeb OCR that reads the price as 3.19 is a misread of the printed 3.39. The correct answer is 3.39 dollars.
