# Shopping — 1959 Sears Gun Rack (reviewer fix)

## Prompt
Sears put out a Christmas catalog in the same calendar year Hawaii became a U.S. state. On the children’s cap-gun and play-weapons pages there is a Western-style Gun Rack that is advertised as teaching neatness. That rack is stained pine with a burned-in western design, has eight hooks for four rifles and three pegs for hats or pistols, measures about 20 inches wide and 20 1/2 inches high, ships partly assembled, and carries a Sears catalog number in the 79 N series. What cash price is printed for that gun rack?

## Answer
3.39 dollars

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Hawaii admitted as a state date"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/hawaii-statehood
Hawaii entered the Union on August 21, 1959. The Sears Christmas catalog from that same calendar year is the 1959 book.

Search "1959 Sears Christmas Book christmas.musetechnical"

Fetch https://christmas.musetechnical.com/ShowCatalog/1959-Sears-Christmas-Book
Open the 1959 Sears Christmas Book on Muse Technical.

Fetch https://christmas.musetechnical.com/ShowCatalogPage/1959-Sears-Christmas-Book/416
Printed page 416 is in the children’s cap-gun / play-weapons section. Find the Western-style Gun Rack that teaches neatness: stained pine with a burned-in western design, eight hooks for four rifles, three pegs for hats or pistols, about 20 inches wide and 20 1/2 inches high, partly assembled, catalog 79 N 02683. The cash price on that listing is 3.39 dollars. Skip other toy prices on the same page (for example 2.89 dollars or 1.93 dollars).

Final answer
3.39 dollars
