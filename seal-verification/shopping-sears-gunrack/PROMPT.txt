Sears put out a Christmas catalog in the same calendar year Hawaii became a U.S. state. On the children’s cap-gun and play-weapons pages there is a Western-style Gun Rack that is advertised as teaching neatness. That rack is stained pine with a burned-in western design, has eight hooks for four rifles and three pegs for hats or pistols, measures about 20 inches wide and 20 1/2 inches high, ships partly assembled, and carries a Sears catalog number in the 79 N series.
