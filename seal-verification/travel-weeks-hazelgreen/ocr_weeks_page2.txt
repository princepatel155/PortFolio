The first contract for construction on the Interstate System using
funds authorized by the 1956 Act was awarded by the Missouri State highway
department on August 2, only slightly more than one month after enactment
of the legislation. The project provided for 2.6 miles of divided highway
on U.S. 66 between Lebanon and Hazelgreen, Missouri. Other projects
for improvement of the Interstate System in Missouri also have been
awarded to contract, so that the State is now placing under construction
six projects costing $3.8 million in Federal and State funds under the
provisions of the 1956 Act.
