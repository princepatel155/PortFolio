# Travel — Weeks letter Lebanon–Hazelgreen project length (2.6 miles)

## Prompt
In Secretary of Commerce Sinclair Weeks’s August 9, 1956 letter to President Eisenhower reporting early progress under the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956, Weeks identified the first Interstate System construction contract awarded by the Missouri State highway department using funds authorized by that Act as a divided-highway project on U.S. 66 between Lebanon and Hazelgreen, Missouri. What length in miles did Weeks give for that project?

## Answer
2.6 miles

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Eisenhower Federal Aid Highway Act 1956 Interstate Highway System"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/interstate-highway-system
On the Eisenhower Library Interstate Highway System page, in the introductory narrative paragraph (the prose block under the page heading, before the document list), confirm that President Eisenhower signed the Federal Aid Highway Act on June 29, 1956. In the document list, open the letter from Sinclair Weeks to President Eisenhower regarding improvement of the Interstate Highway System, August 9, 1956.

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/online-documents/interstate-highway-system/1956-08-09-weeks-to-dde.pdf
Open the two-page scanned Weeks letter (about 328 KB). On page 1, confirm it is on Secretary of Commerce letterhead, addressed to The President, and concerns progress under the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956 with particular reference to the Interstate Highway System.

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/online-documents/interstate-highway-system/1956-08-09-weeks-to-dde.pdf#page=2
On page 2, read the paragraph beginning "The first contract for construction on the Interstate System using funds authorized by the 1956 Act." Confirm Weeks states that the Missouri State highway department awarded that first contract on August 2, and that "The project provided for 2.6 miles of divided highway on U.S. 66 between Lebanon and Hazelgreen, Missouri." Record 2.6 miles. Do not substitute later FHWA retellings that assign 13.3 miles to the Laclede County / U.S. 66 award, and do not relocate the 2.6-mile figure to the St. Charles County U.S. 40 / Mark Twain Expressway project.

Final answer
2.6 miles
