## 1. Prompt
In Secretary of Commerce Sinclair Weeks’s August 9, 1956 letter to President Eisenhower reporting early progress under the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956, Weeks identified the first Interstate System construction contract awarded by the Missouri State highway department using funds authorized by that Act as a divided-highway project on U.S. 66 between Lebanon and Hazelgreen, Missouri. What length in miles did Weeks give for that project?

## 2. Answer
2.6 miles

## 5. Response Failure Justification
ChatGPT answers 13.3 miles for the first Missouri Interstate contract on the U.S. 66 / Laclede County corridor, following later FHWA anniversary retellings of the August 2, 1956 award. Sinclair Weeks’s August 9, 1956 letter to Eisenhower instead states on page 2 that the first contract using 1956 Act funds “provided for 2.6 miles of divided highway on U.S. 66 between Lebanon and Hazelgreen, Missouri.” A second common decoy relocates the 2.6-mile figure to the St. Charles County U.S. 40 / Mark Twain Expressway project (first construction start in later FHWA accounts), which is not what Weeks wrote for the Lebanon–Hazelgreen U.S. 66 project the prompt asks about.
