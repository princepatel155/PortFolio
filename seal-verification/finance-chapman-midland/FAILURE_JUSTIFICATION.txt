The model names the wrong Midland National Bank officer from the August 22, 1959 Commercial West note. On printed page 8 of that issue, Midland National Bank of Minneapolis elected William R. Chapman president to succeed Arnulf Ueland, who was advanced to the newly created office of chairman of the board and continued as chief executive officer. Naming Arnulf Ueland (or another same-page officer) answers the predecessor/chairman role, not the new presidency asked for. The correct answer is William R. Chapman.
