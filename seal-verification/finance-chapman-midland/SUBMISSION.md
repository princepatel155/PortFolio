# Finance — William R. Chapman (Midland National Bank presidency)

## Prompt
In the mid twentieth century Upper Midwest banking and business weekly Commercial West, the issue dated the Saturday after Hawaii became a state carried a Twin Cities bank personnel note that Midland National Bank of Minneapolis had elected a new president to succeed the officer who was advanced to the newly created office of chairman of the board while continuing as chief executive officer. Who was named to that Midland National Bank presidency in the note?

## Answer
William R. Chapman
