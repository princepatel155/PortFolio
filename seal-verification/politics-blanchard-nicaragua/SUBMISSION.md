# Politics — Louis F. Blanchard (Cárdenas escort / Nicaragua)

## Prompt
Four weeks after Alaska entered the Union, the Assistant Secretary of State for Inter-American Affairs wrote the U.S. Ambassador in Nicaragua that the Ambassador's Washington consultation should wait until a named Department officer finished a temporary assignment as Departmental escort for a former Mexican president. Who was that Department officer?

## Answer
Louis F. Blanchard

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Alaska admitted as a state date Eisenhower Library"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/alaska-statehood
On the Eisenhower Library Alaska Statehood page, in the introductory narrative paragraph (the prose block under the page heading, before the document list), confirm the sentence that on January 3, 1959 President Eisenhower signed the official proclamation admitting Alaska as the 49th state. That date was a Saturday. Four weeks later is Saturday, January 31, 1959.

Search "FRUS 1958-1960 Nicaragua Rubottom Whelan January 31 1959"

Fetch https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v05mSupp/ch12
In the Office of the Historian FRUS 1958–1960, Volume V Microfiche Supplement, Nicaragua chapter list, open NI–11: Letter from the Assistant Secretary of State for Inter-American Affairs (Rubottom) to the Ambassador in Nicaragua (Whelan), Washington, January 31, 1959. The chapter listing title does not name Blanchard or the escort assignment.

Fetch https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v05mSupp/ni_d11
Open the January 31, 1959 letter. In the opening body paragraph, locate: "as soon as Louis Blanchard finishes his temporary assignment as Departmental escort officer for ex-President Lásaro Cárdenas."

Fetch https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v05mSupp/persons
Confirm the List of Persons entry: "Blanchard, Louis F., Officer in Charge of Mexican Affairs, Bureau of Inter-American Affairs, Department of State, until December 1958; thereafter Counselor for Consular Affairs of the Embassy in Nicaragua." That index entry omits the temporary Cárdenas escort assignment named in NI–11. Filter out Thomas Whelan (Ambassador addressee), Henry L. Taylor / C. Allan Stewart (Nicaragua desk / OAP officers in the same volume), and inventable wrong "escort" names.

Fetch https://history.state.gov/departmenthistory/people/whelan-thomas-edward
Confirm Thomas Edward Whelan was Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary to Nicaragua (credentials November 3, 1951; left post March 22, 1961)—the letter's addressee, not the escort officer.

Final answer
Louis F. Blanchard
