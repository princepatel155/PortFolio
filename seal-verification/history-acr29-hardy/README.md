# Statutes of California (1959) — ACR No. 29 / Resolution Chapter 48

Open verification package for the History prompt (Short Hills, New Jersey daughter in the Hardy memorial transmission clause).

## Why the loadable extract exists

The official Chief Clerk Volume 2 file  
`https://clerk.assembly.ca.gov/sites/clerk.assembly.ca.gov/files/archive/Statutes/1959/59Vol2_Chapters.pdf`  
is ~143 MB / 2,812 pages. Many in-browser viewers cannot open it to printed pp. 5483–5484.  

The Golden Trajectory therefore records the transmission clause from a **2-page loadable extract of those exact official pages** (PDF pages 2461–2462), archived at:

https://web.archive.org/web/20260723180522id_/https://litter.catbox.moe/9ahg51.pdf

Official navigation still uses the Chief Clerk Measures + Index PDFs (small, open in-browser).

## Verbatim transmission clause (printed p. 5484)

> Resolved, That the Chief Clerk of the Assembly be directed to transmit suitably prepared copies of this resolution to Dr. Hardy's widow, Mary Hardy, and his four daughters, Mrs. Anne Davis, Mrs. Mary Hobbs of Washington, D. C., Mrs. Millison Cutler Doble of Sausalito, and Mrs. Esther Pomeroy Winterer of Short Hills, New Jersey.
