The model invents a capital-structure total that does not match the Western State Bank note in the September 5, 1959 Commercial West. On printed page 13 of that issue, the State Banking Board approval note for Western State Bank at Sioux Falls lists common capital 150,200 dollars, surplus 25,000 dollars, and undivided profits 25,001.58 dollars for a total of 200,201.58 dollars. Naming a component line (150,200 dollars / 25,000 dollars / 25,001.58 dollars) or a rounded 200,000 dollars is not the printed total. The correct answer is 200201.58 dollars.
