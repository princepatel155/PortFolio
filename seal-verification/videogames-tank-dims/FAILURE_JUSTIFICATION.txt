The model reports the wrong console size for the Sears Tele-Games Tank unit in the 1977 Sears Christmas Book. On printed page 5, the Tank listing (battlefield fun for 2 players; two joystick controls with firing buttons; catalog 6 C 99728) gives CONSOLE dimensions of 8 1/2 x 12 x 2 1/4 inches. Naming the same-page Motocross Sports console size (9 1/2 x 15 x 5 inches), inventing a nearby size, or substituting the Tank cash price (58.95 dollars) or shipping weight (4 pounds 8 ounces) is not the length-by-width-by-height console size asked for. The correct answer is 8 1/2 x 12 x 2 1/4 inches.
