# Travel — St. Lambert Lock arrival, June 26, 1959

## Answer
**10:50 a.m.**

## Why this stumps
White House Seaway opening schedule (June 17, 1959 memo) lists many nearby times: Columbine at St. Hubert’s **10:05**, Royal Tent/cars **10:20**, leave St. Hubert’s **10:30**, ceremony **11:00**, Britannia **11:30**. Arrival at St. Lambert Lock is **10:50 a.m.** Distinct from burned July 11, 1958 Eisenhower Lock **10:52 a.m.**
