# Sports — Sport Behind the Iron Curtain pamphlet

## Prompt
In the calendar year before the Olympic Games of the XVI Olympiad held in Melbourne, a U.S. Operations Coordinating Board cultural-committee discussion of Soviet professionalism at those Games suggested that USIA distribute a British pamphlet on an unattributable basis. What was the title of that pamphlet?

## Answer
Sport Behind the Iron Curtain

## Golden Trajectory
Step 1 — Identify which Melbourne Games and the calendar year before them
Search: "XVI Olympiad Melbourne Olympedia"
Fetch: https://www.olympedia.org/editions/14
Verify: On the Olympedia 1956 Summer Olympics overview, confirm “Number and Year XVI / 1956” and “Host city Melbourne, Australia.” The Olympic Games of the XVI Olympiad in Melbourne are therefore the 1956 Games, so the calendar year before those Games is 1955.

Step 2 — Discover the OCB cultural-committee discussion from the prompt constraints
Search: "OCB Cultural Committee USSR 1956 Olympics USIA pamphlet"
Fetch: https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/declassified-documents-fiscal-year-2012
Verify: On the Eisenhower Library Declassified Documents — Fiscal Year 2012 page, under the Cultural Programs heading, locate the listing “Memo of conversation between Armed Forces Professional Entertainment Branch and Defense Representative on OCB Cultural Committee re USSR and 1956 Olympics, November 3, 1955.” This is the Operations Coordinating Board cultural-committee discussion tied to those Games; the November 3, 1955 date falls in the calendar year before the 1956 Melbourne Games. The prompt does not name this library — the OCB / 1956 Olympics / USIA search discovers it.

Step 3 — Read the British pamphlet title from the memorandum
Fetch: https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/online-documents/declassified/fy-2012/1955-11-03.pdf
Verify: Open the one-page November 3, 1955 memorandum. In the suggested-actions list, locate the USIA pamphlet clause:

USIA - Distribute British pamphlet, "Sport Behind the Iron Curtain" on unattributable basis.

Record Sport Behind the Iron Curtain. Do not invent a title such as "The Amateur Racket."

✅ Final answer
Sport Behind the Iron Curtain
