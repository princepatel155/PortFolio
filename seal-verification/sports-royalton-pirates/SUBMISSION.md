# Sports — Royalton Pirates (Eagan May 11, 1958 progress report)

## Prompt
In Edward P. F. Eagan's People-to-People Sports Committee Progress Report listed as May 11, 1958 in the Eisenhower Presidential Library People-to-People online documents, Eagan cited a big-brother correspondence between the Pittsburgh Pirates baseball club and another baseball club in Southern Rhodesia, Africa. What was the name of that African club?

## Answer
Royalton Pirates

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Eisenhower Library People-to-People Sports Committee"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/people-people-program
On the Eisenhower Library People-to-People Program page, in the introductory narrative paragraphs (the prose under the page heading, before the document list), confirm that the program included a Sports Committee among about forty committees. In the document list, open "People-to-People Sports Committee Progress Report. May 11, 1958."

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/file/people_to_people_BinderE.pdf
Open the nine-page scanned Progress Report from Edward P. F. Eagan, Chairman (about 1.6 MB). Early pages discuss the Pakistan cricket visit and other committee projects; they do not name the African baseball club.

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/file/people_to_people_BinderE.pdf#page=9
On page 9, locate Eagan's example of baseball correspondence: "The Pittsburgh Pirates baseball club has struck up correspondence with the Royalton Pirates baseball club of Southern Rodesia, Africa--a sort of big brother-little brother relationship which may well be an example worthy of emulation." Record Royalton Pirates. Do not invent another African club name, and do not substitute Pittsburgh Pirates.

Final answer
Royalton Pirates
