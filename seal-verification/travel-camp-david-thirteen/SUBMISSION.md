# Travel — Camp David staff cabins sleep thirteen guests

## Prompt
On the Eisenhower Presidential Library’s Camp David online-documents page, open the Guidebook to Camp David. According to that guidebook, Sycamore, Hawthorne, Walnut, and Laurel cabins in the White House Staff area provide sleeping accommodations for a maximum of how many guests?

## Answer
thirteen guests
