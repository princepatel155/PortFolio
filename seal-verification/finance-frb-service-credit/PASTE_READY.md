## Write the Prompt
In the Federal Reserve Bulletin issued for December of the calendar year Hawaii was admitted as a U.S. state, the consumer-credit estimates include a footnote that Hawaii enters the series beginning with August of that year. On the consumer credit by major parts table in that issue, the latest month shown is October. The table gives estimated short- and intermediate-term credit outstanding in millions of dollars and breaks noninstalment credit into single-payment loans, charge accounts, and service credit. What amount does that October month-end row list for noninstalment service credit, in millions of dollars?

## Write the Answer
2876 million dollars

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Hawaii admitted as a state date"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/hawaii-statehood
Confirm Hawaii was admitted on August 21, 1959. The Federal Reserve Bulletin for December of that calendar year is the December 1959 issue.

Search "Federal Reserve Bulletin December 1959 FRASER"

Fetch https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/FRB/1950s/frb_121959.pdf
Open the December 1959 Federal Reserve Bulletin on FRASER.

Fetch https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/FRB/1950s/frb_121959.pdf#page=75
Printed page 1524, table “CONSUMER CREDIT, BY MAJOR PARTS,” amounts in millions of dollars. Footnote 3 states data for Hawaii begin with August 1959. The latest monthly row is October 1959. Under Noninstalment credit, Service credit for that October row is 2,876. Filter out same-row decoys: total credit 49,872; instalment total 38,421; noninstalment total 11,451; single-payment loans 4,050; charge accounts 4,525; and September service credit 2,914. Record: 2876 million dollars.

Final answer
2876 million dollars
