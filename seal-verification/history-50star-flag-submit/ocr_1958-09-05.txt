MEMORANDUM FOR
GOVERNOR ADAMS
There is need for finally determining the procedure under which the
fF Flag will be modified to contain forty-nine stars. This has been dis-
cussed on a preliminary basis with the President earlier this summer,
at which time the President was interested in having some sort of a
group established to review the matter briefly and advise him appro-
priately.
There has been no fixed tradition for accomplishing the changes in the
past. Admiral Furlong's letter of June 8, 1953, to the President pro-
vides an accurate summary of the procedures followed heretofore
(Tab A).
An advisory group, when first discussed with the President, might
have taken the form of (1) interdepartmental; (2) prominent public
citizens; (3) joint -- including Congressional membership. Since
then, Congress declined to move in on this and both Speaker Rayburn
and Joe Martin have expressed their concurrence in having the Presi-
dent handle this strictly from the Executive Branch.
Defense Department very early made a recommendation to the President
based on the traditional military interest and activity in modifications
of the Flag. Consequently, a great deal of the basic work has already
been accomplished and it would be relatively easy for a more broadly
based group to review this work and render advice to the President.
This suggests the desirability of forming the group exclusively of
Administration officials. Appropriate membership would consist of
the top men or at least an Assistant Secretary from the following:
