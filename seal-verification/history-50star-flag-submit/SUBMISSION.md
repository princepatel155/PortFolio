# History — 50-star flag committee submission date

## Prompt
In the calendar year when Alaska was admitted to the Union as a U.S. state, White House aides restarted work on a new national flag design for the added star. According to the Eisenhower Presidential Library online account of that design process, a committee later formally submitted designs for a fifty-star flag as well. On what date did that committee formally submit the fifty-star flag designs?

## Answer
August 17, 1959

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Alaska statehood date Eisenhower"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/alaska-statehood
Alaska became a state on January 3, 1959. That is the calendar year for the flag-design work in the prompt.

Search "Eisenhower Library design 49 and 50 star flags"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/design-49-and-50-star-flags
Library page on the 49- and 50-star flag design process. It says the Committee formally submitted designs for the 50-star flag on August 17, 1959. Nearby dates on the same page are different events: December 30, 1958 (49-star designs submitted), January 3, 1959 (Executive Order 10798 for the 49-star flag), August 21, 1959 (Executive Order 10834 for the 50-star flag), and July 4, 1960 (50-star flag first raised at Fort McHenry).

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/hawaii-statehood
Hawaii admission is also August 21, 1959, which matches the 50-star executive-order date on the flag page and is a decoy for the committee submission date.

Final answer
August 17, 1959
