The model names the wrong person from the Western State Bank approval note in the September 5, 1959 Commercial West. On printed page 13 of that issue, officers of the new Sioux Falls bank had not yet been elected, but it was understood that the executive officer would be Oscar Brosz, former vice president of the Dakota State Bank at Tripp, S. D. Naming an incorporator such as T. M. Reardon or B. Scott Reardon, or commissioner Gordan Maxam, is not the understood executive officer. The correct answer is Oscar Brosz.
