The model invented a U.S. funds amount that does not appear on the enclosed first tolls assessment form. On page 2 of the May 1, 1959 Castle packet in the Eisenhower Library Seaway collection, the Tolls Assessment shows 20.68 dollars payable to the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation in U.S. funds on the St. Lawrence GRT line. The form does not list 115.80 dollars as that U.S. payable amount. The correct answer is 20.68 dollars.
