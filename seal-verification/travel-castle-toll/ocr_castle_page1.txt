Dear Mr. President:

The enclosed billing is a copy of the first invoice
mailed from the Tolls Office of the two Seaway entities.

The SIMCOE came in light, so the only charge was
that against gross registered tonnage. This accounts for the
small amount in dollars under our tariff schedule.

During the first three and one-half days, beginning
April 25 after official opening, we transited through the Seaway
168 vessels, of which 67% were in ballast. Total billings were
$112, 595. 73.

I thought you might enjoy this document as an
historic record, and possibly place it in the Abilene library.

Renesas yours,

Lewis G. Castle

Administrator
