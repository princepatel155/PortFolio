# Travel — Castle first Seaway toll invoice U.S. amount ($20.68)

## Prompt
Lewis G. Castle, Administrator of the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, wrote President Eisenhower on May 1, 1959 enclosing a copy of the first invoice mailed from the Tolls Office of the two Seaway entities. On that enclosed tolls assessment form, what amount in U.S. funds is shown as payable to the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation?

## Answer
$20.68

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Eisenhower Library St. Lawrence Seaway"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/st-lawrence-seaway
On the Eisenhower Library St. Lawrence Seaway page, in the introductory narrative paragraph (the prose block under the page heading, before the document list), confirm that on May 13, 1954 President Eisenhower signed the Seaway Act authorizing a deep-water 114-mile navigation channel in the St. Lawrence River between Montreal and Ogdensburg, New York. In the document list, open the entry titled along the lines of Letter, Lewis Castle to President Eisenhower enclosing receipt of first toll assessment of the St. Lawrence Seaway dated April 27, 1959 (letter date May 1, 1959).

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/online-documents/st-lawrence-seaway/1959-05-01-castle-to-dde.pdf
Open the two-page scanned Castle packet. On page 1, confirm the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation letterhead (Seaway Circle, Massena, N.Y.), the May 1, 1959 date, and that Castle encloses a copy of the first invoice mailed from the Tolls Office of the two Seaway entities. Note his separate period statistic that during the first three and one-half days beginning April 25, total billings were $112,595.73 — do not treat that page-1 figure as the invoice amount asked for.

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/online-documents/st-lawrence-seaway/1959-05-01-castle-to-dde.pdf#page=2
On page 2, read the Tolls Assessment form. Confirm the St. Lawrence GRT line shows 1783 tons at a 4¢ rate, with $50.64 payable to The St. Lawrence Seaway Authority (Canadian funds) and $20.68 payable to the Saint Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation (U.S. funds). Record $20.68. Do not substitute the Canadian-funds column, and do not substitute Castle’s page-1 multi-day total billings figure.

Final answer
$20.68
