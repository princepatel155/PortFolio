# Travel — Conger: United States built 2/7ths

## Prompt
On the Eisenhower Presidential Library’s St. Lawrence Seaway online-documents page, open the November 19, 1958 memorandum from Clement E. Conger to Mr. Stephens about physical arrangements for the President and Queen’s Seaway opening. According to that memorandum, what fraction of the Seaway has been built by the United States?

## Answer
2/7ths
