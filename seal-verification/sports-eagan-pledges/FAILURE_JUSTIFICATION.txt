The model answers from an earlier dollar figure in the same Progress Report instead of the December luncheon pledges total. On page 4 of the May 11, 1958 Eagan Progress Report in the Eisenhower Library People-to-People collection, paragraph 7 states that the committee secured pledges totalling 6500 dollars from a fund-raising luncheon staged the previous December in New York. Page 1's 15000-dollar Pakistan cricket project figure and page 2's 2000000-dollar H.R. 11252 appropriation are not that luncheon pledges total. The correct answer is 6500 dollars.
