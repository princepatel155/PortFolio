MEMORANDUM

TO: Members of the People-to-People Sports Committee, Inc.
FROM: Edward P. F. Eagan, Chairman

SUBJECT: Progress Report

1, Your attention is invited to this special stationery
printed by our standing committee for the sport of Cricket,
and being used in the promotion of a two week visit to the
U. S. by 18 all-star cricketeers from Pakistan. This
represents a $15,000 project which the committee expects
to finance with the gate receipts, etc., of four games the
Pakistanis will play against all-star delegations from
various Eastern cricket leagues. One game will be played
in Philadelphia on May 3rd, and three in New York on May 4th,
May 10th and May 11th. How many tickets at $1.50 each would

you like? The enclosure will fill you in on the details.
