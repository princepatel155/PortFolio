The model names 14 x 9 1/4 x 3 3/4 inches as the console dimensions for the Sears Tele-Games Video Arcade in the Wish Book that introduced that cartridge system. That measurement does not match the printed listing. On printed page 2 of the 1977 Sears Christmas Book, Video Arcade (Target Fun included; 2 joystick and 2 paddle controls; catalog 6 C 99743) describes a streamlined impact-resistant plastic console 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 in. high. Secondary Internet Archive OCR garbles the same line as 14x914x3%4, which invites inventing 9 1/4 instead of 9 1/2 (and mangling 3 3/4). The correct answer is 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 inches.
