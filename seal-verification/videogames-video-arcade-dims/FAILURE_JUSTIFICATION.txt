The model invents a console size that does not match the Video Arcade listing in the 1977 Sears Christmas Wish Book. On printed page 2 of that catalog, the Tele-Games Video Arcade unit that includes the Target Fun cartridge with two joysticks and two paddle controls (catalog 6 C 99743) lists console size 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 in. high. The response’s 14 1/2 x 9 x 4 inches figure is taken from a secondary owner’s manual and is not the size printed for that item. The correct answer is 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 inches.
