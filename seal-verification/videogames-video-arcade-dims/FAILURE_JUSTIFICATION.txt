The model invents console dimensions that do not match the Video Arcade listing in the 1977 Sears Christmas Wish Book. On printed page 2 of that catalog, the Tele-Games Video Arcade unit that includes the Target Fun cartridge with two joystick and two paddle controls (catalog 6 C 99743) lists console size 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 in. high. The response’s 14 x 9 1/4 x 3 3/4 inches figure misreads the printed fractions (Internet Archive OCR garbles that line as 14x914x3%4). The correct answer is 14 x 9 1/2 x 3 3/4 inches.
