# Reviewer Justifications — Video Games / Tele-Games Video Arcade shipping weight

## Domain fit
Video Arcade is the Sears Tele-Games cartridge console (Atari VCS / 2600 OEM) introduced in the 1977 Christmas Wish Book games section. Shipping weight is a printed catalog order-info field on that debut listing.

## Why invent fail is meaningful
Secondary Wishbook HTML OCR garbles the line as “6 pounds S ounces,” and web synthesis invents 6 pounds 8 ounces. Same page also lists accessory weights (4 ounces paddles; 6 ounces antenna) and the well-known 178.95 dollars cash price—none is the asked shipping weight. Michael Current’s Atari timeline records the 178.95 dollars price but not this shipping weight.
