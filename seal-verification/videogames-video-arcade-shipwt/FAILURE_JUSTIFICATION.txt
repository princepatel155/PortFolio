The model invents a shipping weight that does not appear on the Video Arcade console listing in the 1977 Sears Christmas Wish Book. On printed page 2 of that catalog, the Tele-Games Video Arcade unit that includes the Target Fun cartridge with two joystick and two paddle controls (catalog 6 C 99743) lists shipping weight 6 pounds 5 ounces. The response’s 6 pounds 8 ounces figure is not the weight printed for that item. The correct answer is 6 pounds 5 ounces.
