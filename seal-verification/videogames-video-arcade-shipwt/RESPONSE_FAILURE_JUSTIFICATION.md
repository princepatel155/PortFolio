The model names 6 pounds 8 ounces as the shipping weight for the Sears Tele-Games Video Arcade console in the Wish Book that introduced that cartridge system. That amount does not appear on the Video Arcade listing. On printed page 2 of the 1977 Sears Christmas Book, Video Arcade (Target Fun cartridge included; 2 joystick and 2 paddle controls; catalog 6 C 99743) lists shipping weight 6 pounds 5 ounces. The model therefore fabricates a nearby-looking weight—consistent with garbled secondary OCR that misreads “5” as “S”/“8”—instead of reading the printed figure.
