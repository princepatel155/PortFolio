# Finance — E.4 Hawaii capital accounts

## Prompt
At the close of the calendar year in which Hawaii was admitted as a U.S. state, the Federal Reserve Board published a year-end statistical release that tabulated principal assets and liabilities of all banks in the United States and other areas by state. On the December 31 table from that release, Hawaii appears in the alphabetical state list between Georgia and Idaho. The table heading states that amounts are in thousands of dollars. According to the Hawaii row on that all-banks table, what figure is reported for capital accounts, expressed in full dollars rather than in thousands?

## Answer
59590000 dollars

## Golden Trajectory
Search "Hawaii admitted as a state date"

Fetch https://www.eisenhowerlibrary.gov/research/online-documents/hawaii-statehood
Confirm Hawaii was admitted on August 21, 1959. The close of that calendar year is December 31, 1959.

Search "Federal Reserve E.4 all banks by states December 31 1959 FRASER"

Fetch https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/releases/e4/e4_19591231.pdf
Open the Board of Governors E.4 release for December 31, 1959 on FRASER.

Fetch https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/releases/e4/e4_19591231.pdf#page=1
Sheet E.4(a), “ALL BANKS IN THE UNITED STATES AND OTHER AREAS — PRINCIPAL ASSETS AND LIABILITIES, BY STATES, DECEMBER 31, 1959,” amounts in thousands of dollars. In the state list, Hawaii sits between Georgia and Idaho. On the Hawaii row, Capital accounts is 59,590. Filter out same-row decoys (total assets 729,306; total deposits 659,474; loans and investments 574,445; number of banks 11) and neighbor-state capital totals (Georgia 258,814; Idaho 44,331). Convert thousands to full dollars: 59,590 × 1,000 = 59,590,000. Record: 59590000 dollars.

Final answer
59590000 dollars
