The model names the wrong officer from the Northwest Security National Bank personnel note in the August 15, 1959 Commercial West. On that note, amid the retirement of board chairman and chief executive officer J. Virgil Lowe, John V. Krastins was promoted from assistant cashier to cashier. Naming Jack McMillan (named vice president), David G. Jewett (former cashier moved to the Huron branch as assistant vice president), or Lowe himself is not the cashier promotion asked for. The correct answer is John V. Krastins.
